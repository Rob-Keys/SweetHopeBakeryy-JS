# Image codec notices

The browser-side upload converters use these WebAssembly codecs. Their corresponding license texts are in this directory.

- `avif_enc.wasm` and `avif_enc_mt.wasm`: `@jsquash/avif` 2.1.1, Apache-2.0.
- `gif2webp.wasm`: `@libwebp-wasm/gif2webp` 1.0.8, MIT.
- HEIC decoding fallback: `libheif-js` 1.23.5 and its bundled libheif codec, LGPL-3.0. Source: https://github.com/catdad-experiments/libheif-js and https://github.com/strukturag/libheif.
